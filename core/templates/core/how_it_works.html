How DocBot Works

Discover how DocBot guides you from symptoms to the right care in just a few simple steps.

Step 1

Share Your Symptoms

Start a friendly chat with DocBot. Describe how you feel in plain language—just like you're talking to a friend. Our AI uses natural language processing to ask the right follow-up questions and understand the full context of your health.

Interactive AI Chat: Easy conversation with text and options.
Intelligent Follow-ups: Smart questions tailored to your inputs.
24/7 Availability: Get guidance the moment you feel unwell.
Hi! What symptoms are you experiencing?
I have a headache and fever since yesterday
Step 2

Smart Clinical Evaluation

DocBot’s AI analyzes your symptoms against clinical logic to evaluate the urgency of your case. You’ll receive immediate guidance: whether it's simple advice for home rest or a recommendation to visit a specialist for further examination.

Urgency Assessment: Knowing if your case is mild or needs attention.
Clinical Data Mapping: Your symptoms are organized into a professional summary.
Direct Doctor Notification: Your summarized case is sent directly to your chosen specialist.
Analyzing your symptoms...
Your case is moderate. Recommended: consult a specialist.
Step 3

Match with Top Specialists

If a visit is needed, DocBot doesn't leave you searching. Our system identifies the exact internal medicine sub-specialty you need and recommends the top 3 verified doctors in that field to ensure you get the best care.

Smart Specialty Matching: Automatic routing to the right organ-specific expert.
Top 3 Recommendations: Hand-picked consultants based on your needs.
Integrated EHR: Your doctor reviews your AI-generated history before you even walk into the clinic.
